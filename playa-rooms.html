Perfect Location

Our Playa del Carmen hostel is ideally situated in the heart of the city, providing easy access to all the best attractions and experiences.

Shopping & Dining

Just 2 minutes walking distance from the famous 5th Avenue, with its countless shops, restaurants, and bars. Experience the vibrant heart of Playa del Carmen right at your doorstep.

Beautiful Beaches

Only a 5-minute walk to Playa del Carmen's stunning white-sand beaches. Swim in the crystal-clear turquoise waters of the Caribbean Sea or simply relax under the sun.

Cozumel Ferry

Just 10 minutes walking distance to the ferry terminal for day trips to Cozumel Island. Explore the world-famous coral reefs and enjoy some of the best scuba diving and snorkeling in the world.
